## 1. Game idea

- `words` contains the possible secret words.
- `secret` is the chosen word for one round.
- `guessed` stores letters the player already tried.
- The game prints `_` for letters not yet guessed.
- Correct guesses are revealed in the word.
- `wrong` counts incorrect guesses.
- The game ends when the player wins or reaches 6 wrong guesses.
